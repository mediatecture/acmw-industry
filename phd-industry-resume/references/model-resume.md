# Model resume (anonymised, annotated)

A real resume from someone with a design-related PhD who moved from academia into public
programs, consulting and industry research leadership. Names, employers, places and dates are
removed, and **numbers have been changed** so it can't be traced. The structure, level of detail
and phrasing are real. Use it to show **structure, level of detail and how outcomes are phrased**.
Never copy its text or numbers into a user's resume.

This person had about 15 years of experience, so 3 pages is justified. A fresh graduate should
aim for 1–2.

## What to take from it

1. **Structure:** contact → summary → skills → experience (most recent first, PhD included as a
   role) → education → achievements → media. Experience starts on page 1.
2. **Every role has a one-line scope** (team, budget, number of projects) before the bullets.
3. **Bold lead-in + evidence** makes bullets skimmable.
4. **Money and scale** appear throughout: funding secured, budgets managed, people reached.
5. **Outcomes, not duties:** "secured", "rebuilt", "adopted", "won".
6. **Publications are outcomes**, not a list. The full record lives on the portfolio.
7. **Resumes keep improving:** the summary went from filler to plain and specific, and the newest
   role still needs numbers. Use these to show users that improvement is always possible.
